Privacy Policy — The Drive-Thru Diet

Last updated: September 2026

The Drive-Thru Diet app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, device data, or usage data of any kind. There are no user accounts, no analytics tools, no advertising SDKs, and no network requests of any kind — the app works entirely offline using data built into the app itself.

Nothing you enter into the app (your calorie or protein targets, chain selections, or preferences) ever leaves your device. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party, because the app has no way to send anything anywhere.

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at drivethrudiet@gmail.com.
